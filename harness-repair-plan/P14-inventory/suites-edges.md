  Packages went only into the sandbox venvs in the scratchpad: nothing went into the repo or the real HOME, and no pinned hashes were used. Anyone rerunning a replay offline should put that dir, or an installed xdist, on PYTHONPATH first.
- **Files the suites wrote inside the repo.** All are git-ignored `__pycache__` dirs, despite PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE: hooks spawned with a reset environment, and the sandbox bytecode build. I restored nothing:
